Chè Frè ak Sè nan Kris la,
Dear Brothers and Sisters in Christ,

Nou kontan mete ti gid devosyonèl sa a nan men w pou ede w grandi espirityèlman
chak jou. Se yon kado ki soti nan kè nou pou nou ka bati lafwa nou ansanm.
We are glad to provide this daily devotional guide to help you grow spiritually 
every day. It is a gift from our hearts to build our faith together.

"Paske, kote de oswa twa moun sanble nan non mwen, mwen la nan mitan yo."
- Matye 18:20
"For where two or three are gathered in my name, there am I among them."
- Matthew 18:20

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
JOU 1: KMANSE AVÈK LAFWA / DAY 1: BEGINNING WITH FAITH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[VÈSÈ POU MEDITE / SCRIPTURE REF]
Hébreux 11:1
"Lafwa se yon asirans solid sou sa nou espere, se prèv sou sa nou pa wè."
"Now faith is the assurance of things hoped for, the conviction of things not seen."

[MEDITASYON / THOUGHT FOR THE DAY]
Lafwa se pa sèlman kwè lè tout bagay ap mache byen, men se fè Bondye konfyans
menm lè nou nan mitan gwo tanpèt. Jodi a, kite tout enkyetid ou yo nan men Seyè a.
Faith is not just believing when everything goes well, but trusting God even
in the midst of heavy storms. Today, leave all your worries in the hands of the Lord.

[LAPRIYÈ / PRAYER]
Seyè, ban m fòs pou m mache nan lafwa jodi a. Netwaye kè m epi ban m asirans. Amèn.
Lord, give me strength to walk in faith today. Cleanse my heart and grant me assurance. Amen.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
JOU 2: PREPARASYON POU RETOU LI / DAY 2: PREPARING FOR HIS RETURN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[VÈSÈ POU MEDITE / SCRIPTURE REF]
1 Thessaloniciens 4:16
"Paske, lè sa a, Seyè a li menm va desann soti nan syèl la ak yon gwo rèl..."
"For the Lord himself will descend from heaven with a cry of command..."

[MEDITASYON / THOUGHT FOR THE DAY]
Non egliz nou an, "Parousie," vle di "Retou Seyè a." N'ap viv chak jou avèk
espwa ak preparasyon pou rankontre Sovè nou an nan nwaj yo.
The name of our church, "Parousie," means "The Lord's Return." We live every day
with the hope and preparation to meet our Savior in the clouds.

[LAPRIYÈ / PRAYER]
Jezikri, kenbe m pare. Ede m viv yon lavi ki fè w plezi jiskaske w tounen. Amèn.
Jesus Christ, keep me ready. Help me live a life that pleases You until You return. Amen.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
JOU 3: GRANDI NAN KOMINOTE / DAY 3: GROWING IN FELLOWSHIP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[VÈSÈ POU MEDITE / SCRIPTURE REF]
Psaume 133:1
"Gade ki jan sa bèl, ki jan sa dous pou frè yo viv ansanm nan linite!"
"Behold, how good and pleasant it is when brothers dwell in unity!"

[MEDITASYON / THOUGHT FOR THE DAY]
Nou fèt pou nou grandi ansanm. Sipòte frè ak sè w yo, pote fado yo, epi priye
youn pou lòt avèk senserite.
We are designed to grow together. Support your brothers and sisters, carry
their burdens, and pray for one another with sincerity.

[LAPRIYÈ / PRAYER]
Papa ki nan Syèl la, bati linite nan mitan nou. Ede n renmen lòt moun tankou tèt nou. Amèn.
Heavenly Father, build unity among us. Help us love others as ourselves. Amen.
