**Re: Formal Notice of Project Completion — Parousia Baptist Ministries Website**

Dear Pastor Yvan Dalzon and Leadership Team,

I am pleased to formally notify you that all contracted work on the **Parousia Baptist Ministries** website has been completed, tested, and deployed to production. The site is now live and ready for ongoing use by your congregation and leadership.

---

## Project Scope — Items Completed

All items from our agreed scope of work (June 6 and June 9, 2026) have been delivered:

### Branding & Design

1. Church name updated to **Parousia Baptist Ministries** across the website and admin system.
2. Site color theme set to **light pastel blue**, with an admin Color Adjuster for future adjustments.
3. Hero background updated to remove gold floral elements; custom hero artwork and logo display are now cleanly integrated.
4. Daily devotional verses support a **configurable theme**; when no theme is set, verses are selected at random.

### Navigation & Content Structure

5. Website displays responsively on **mobile phones, tablets, and desktop** computers.
6. **Home** tab includes sub-sections: About Us, Our Beliefs, Our Team (Pastor and Assistant Pastor), and What to Expect.
7. **Ministries** tab added with sub-sections: Women, Men, Children, and Missions.
8. **Community Outreach** tab replaced with a **Blog** tab for the Pastor's weekly point of view.
9. **Administration** tab replaced with a **gear (settings) icon**, visible only to logged-in administrators.

### Security & Administration

10. Admin portal supports **adding additional administrators by email address**. First-time logins and logins from new devices require **email verification (OTP code)**.
11. **Contact form** added for individuals who wish to be contacted by the church.
12. **Prayer wall** added with an anonymous option; public display shows date, request, and requester name (when not anonymous).
